# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Professional installers and sellers of automation systems in Poland, typically aged 40+. They value reliability, technical clarity, and straightforward communication over complex tech trends.

## Product Purpose
To promote the Gaposa brand as an authorized distributor (Cezab Distribution) and showcase their industrial drives and automation products. The goal is to generate interest and inquiries from B2B partners, not to function as an e-commerce shop.

## Brand Personality
Professional, reliable, and approachable. It reflects Italian engineering precision combined with local Polish support. It should feel high-quality ("premium") but grounded and easy to navigate.

## Anti-references
Avoid "over-designed" or overly complex layouts that might confuse non-technical users. Avoid looking like a generic retail hardware store or a cluttered e-commerce site.

## Design Principles
- **Industrial Precision, Human Simplicity**: Present professional-grade technology through a clear, readable, and intuitive interface.
- **Product as the Hero**: High-quality imagery and structured technical data are the primary focus.
- **Trust through Partnership**: Explicitly highlight the relationship between Gaposa (manufacturer) and Cezab Distribution (authorized expert).
- **Legibility First**: Prioritize high contrast, large typography, and comfortable spacing to accommodate users who may be less tech-savvy.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Prioritize high visual clarity and simple navigation patterns. Ensure the interface is usable in various field conditions (e.g., mobile use on-site).
